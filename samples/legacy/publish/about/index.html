About

I'm Mara, a former ferry timetable planner who now spends half the year on trains and the other half in a very small flat in Gothenburg.

Field Notes started as a notebook of things I wanted to remember: the bakery in Trieste that opens at five, the right way to fold a map in the wind, how much rice one pot can really hold.

What you'll find here

  • Slow travel: routes by train, ferry and on foot, with honest timings.
  • Small kitchens: recipes that need one pot and no oven.
  • Long walks: day hikes close to a station.
