---
title: A coast walk from the station
date: 2026-09-21
---

Twelve kilometres along the cliffs, starting and ending at a railway station, with a café exactly halfway. The easiest good walk I know.

<!--more-->

The path is marked with yellow posts. Keep the sea on your left on the way out and you can't get lost. Bring a jacket even in September: the wind on the headland is serious.
