Night train packing list

Everything I take on a sleeper, in one small bag.

  • A light scarf: the air conditioning runs all night.
  • Water and something sweet for the morning.
  • Earplugs, always.
  • A head torch, set to red.
  • Slippers or thick socks for the corridor.
  • Cash in the local currency for the first coffee.
