title: The night train to Trieste
date: 2026-11-02

The sleeper leaves Vienna just before nine and arrives in Trieste as the bakeries open. Here is how to book it, what to bring and where to have breakfast.

<!--more-->

## Booking

Book the couchette six weeks ahead; the four-berth compartments sell out first. A window seat facing forward is worth the small extra fee.

## What to bring

- A light scarf: the air conditioning runs all night.
- Water and something sweet for the morning.
- Earplugs, always.

## Breakfast

Walk five minutes from the station to the harbour. The first bakery with its lights on is the right one.
