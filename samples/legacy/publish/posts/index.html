The night train to Trieste

2 November 2026

The sleeper leaves Vienna just before nine and arrives in Trieste as the bakeries open. Here is how to book it, what to bring and where to have breakfast.

Booking

Book the couchette six weeks ahead; the four-berth compartments sell out first. A window seat facing forward is worth the small extra fee.

What to bring

The short version: a scarf, water, earplugs. The long version is my packing list.

Breakfast

Walk down to the harbour and take the first table in the sun.

One-pot lentils for a hostel kitchen

14 October 2026

Twenty minutes, one pot, four ingredients you can find in any corner shop. This is the dish I cook on the first evening in a new town.

Ingredients

  • 200 g red lentils
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tin of tomatoes
  • A spoon of curry powder

Method

Soften the onion, add the spice, then the lentils, tomatoes and two tins of water. Simmer until thick. Salt at the end.

A coast walk from the station

21 September 2026

Twelve kilometres along the cliffs, starting and ending at a railway station, with a café exactly halfway. The easiest good walk I know.

The path is marked with yellow posts. Keep the sea on your left on the way out and you can't get lost. Bring a jacket even in September: the wind on the headland is serious.
